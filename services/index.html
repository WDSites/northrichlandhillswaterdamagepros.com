Our six services

Water damage is not one job. A burst pipe needs fast extraction, a flood needs sanitizing and a slow leak needs drying and repair. Pick the page that matches what you see, or call (817) 623-9267 and a dispatcher will point you to the right one.

Water Damage Repair

Rebuilding after drying: ceilings, walls, hardwood floors, trim, texture and paint.

Read the page

Emergency Water Removal

Pumps, truck-mounted and portable extraction units for standing water from burst pipes, appliances and flooded rooms.

Read the page

Water Mitigation

Stopping the damage in the first 24 to 72 hours: extraction, drying equipment, moisture mapping and monitoring.

Read the page

Water Damage Cleanup

Debris and wet materials out, surfaces sanitized, odors controlled and contents sorted after the water is stopped.

Read the page

Flood Damage Restoration

Storm, creek and drain flooding, basements and sewage backups handled with contaminated-water safety steps.

Read the page

Mold Remediation

Containment, removal and cleaning after mold growth, with the moisture source fixed first.
