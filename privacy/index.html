Privacy Policy

What this policy covers

This policy explains how North Richland Hills Water Damage Pros handles information when you visit northrichlandhillswaterdamagepros.com or call our line. This site has no forms, so we do not collect information through the website itself.

Phone calls

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes. When you call, we may receive your phone number, the address of the property and the details you share about the water damage. We use that information to dispatch a crew, explain scope and price and follow up on your request.

Website data

Like most websites, our host may log technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. We use it to keep the site working and to understand how it is used. The site loads fonts from Google Fonts and a map from Google Maps, and those services receive your IP address when the page loads.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with crews and partners who need it to complete your job, or when the law requires it.

Your choices

To ask what we hold about you or to request deletion, write to contact@northrichlandhillswaterdamagepros.com. We may need to keep records of completed work.

Changes

We may update this policy. Last updated: October 26, 2026.
