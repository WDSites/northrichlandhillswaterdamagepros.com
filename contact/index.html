How to reach us

Call (817) 623-9267 for emergency water removal, drying, repair or mold questions. The line is open 24/7, including weekends and holidays, and we offer same-day response when crews are available.

We are located at 7001 Boulevard 26, North Richland Hills, TX 76180. You can also write to contact@northrichlandhillswaterdamagepros.com, though for water in the house a phone call is always faster.

What to tell the dispatcher

  • Your street address and the nearest cross street.
  • Where the water came from and whether it is still running.
  • Which rooms are wet and roughly how deep the water is.
  • Whether any water touched outlets, the panel or the ceiling.
