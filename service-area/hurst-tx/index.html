What Hurst tells homeowners about its water and drainage

The City of Hurst reports about 205 miles of water lines and 1,236 fire hydrants. Its water comes from two supply connections with the City of Fort Worth, six city-owned wells and three emergency connections. The 2025 water quality report lists total hardness of 108 to 150 parts per million, which is 6 to 9 grains per gallon.

For an immediate water, wastewater or street problem, Hurst lists a 24/7 Public Works Dispatch line at 817-788-7212, which is the right call if a main or a street drain is the source. Hurst also created a Storm Drainage Utility in 2009 to maintain its drainage system and reduce flooding hazards, so drainage questions go to the city, not to us.
