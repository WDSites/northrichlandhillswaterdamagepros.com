Water Damage Restoration in Iron Horse, North Richland Hills, TX

Iron Horse mixes single-family homes, apartments and commercial property, so water damage here can involve owners, renters and landlords.

A mixed area along Iron Horse Boulevard

The Iron Horse area lies along Iron Horse Boulevard north of Interstate 820, with the North Richland Hills Iron Horse TEX Rail station nearby. The city also has the Iron Horse Golf Course, and the name is used for both the area and the course.

A council of governments station profile lists single-family, multi-family, commercial, industrial, planned development and transit-oriented zoning in the area. If water has gotten into your property, dial (817) 623-9267 and say whether it is a house, apartment or business.

What the planning record says

The station profile lists streets such as Iron Horse Drive, Browning Road, Industrial Park Drive and Boulder Drive, and a park-and-ride with 386 spaces. Within a half mile it counted about 2,670 housing units, roughly 69 percent owner-occupied and 31 percent renter-occupied. The profile is from the 2010s, so treat the numbers as a snapshot.

We found no source describing a flood problem in Iron Horse, and we do not claim one. Neighborhood-specific FEMA flood-zone status not verified for Iron Horse. With so many renters and a mix of building types, who must be notified is often the first practical question.

Safety in the first hour

Apartments and commercial spaces can have shared electrical and plumbing. If you cannot tell where a circuit or valve leads, wait for help. Here is what to do in the meantime.

  • Leave a shared panel or valve alone if you cannot tell which unit it controls.
  • Close the unit shutoff if it is dry and clearly yours, or ask the manager to close the building valve.
  • Do not plug in fans or vacuums near water, and keep cords off wet floors.
  • Photograph the damage, the date and any damaged belongings for the landlord and insurer.
  • Keep visitors and pets out of rooms that hold sewage or drain water.
  • Call the utility or 911 for a gas smell or sparks, then call (817) 623-9267 for the water.

Extraction, drying and containment

The crew extracts standing water, finds hidden moisture with meters and a thermal camera and sets drying equipment. In multi-unit buildings the work often includes the wall or ceiling next to the damaged unit.

The ANSI/IICRC S500 standard is the usual reference. Water from a drain or sewer is handled as contaminated, so do not use household fans or a vacuum on it. Tell the dispatcher about any sewage smell right away.

Insurance and records

Texas Department of Insurance guidance describes sewer or drain backup as an optional endorsement, so a backup may not be covered under a basic policy. A sudden event and a continuous leak are also treated differently.

Renters policies have their own terms, and the landlord's policy usually does not cover a tenant's belongings. Read your policy and ask your insurer. Photos and a written scope help describe the loss.
