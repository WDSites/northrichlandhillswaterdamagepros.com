What to do when water is spreading in Keller

Call right away if water is moving through the ceiling, if more than one room is wet or if a pipe has split. A fast look at water mitigation shows what happens after extraction.

Stop the source

Turn off the main water valve, or the appliance valve if a washer or dishwasher hose is the source.

Power and safety

Do not touch outlets in a wet room, and call the utility or 911 if water is near the electrical panel.

Protect what you can

Move furniture off wet carpet and lift valuables onto counters or beds in dry rooms.

Then call the line

Keller growth and what it means for houses

Keller grew from 827 residents in 1960 to 45,776 in 2020, and the fastest growth came between 1970 and 1990. That means a large share of homes are 35 to 55 years old, while newer subdivisions have filled in around them. Older homes have aging plumbing and roofs, and newer ones can have appliance and supply line failures that nobody notices for days.

City Hall sits in Keller Town Center on Bear Creek Parkway, near the headwaters of Big Bear Creek. If you are in a low spot near a creek or drainage channel, check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask your insurer about flood coverage.
