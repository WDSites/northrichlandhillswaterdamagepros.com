What to do when water is spreading in Colleyville

Call right away if water is flowing, if a ceiling is wet or if a finished lower level is taking on water. Start with the water damage cleanup page if the source is already stopped.

Stop the source

Power and safety

If water is near outlets, a panel or a sump pump, keep out and call the utility or 911 for any hazard.

Protect what you can

How Colleyville grew, and why it matters

Colleyville was originally called Bransford and took the Colleyville name in 1914. It grew from 1,491 people in 1960 to 26,057 in 2020, and much of that growth followed proximity to DFW International Airport. Many houses are larger than average, with more bathrooms and more plumbing runs, so one failed line can reach more finished rooms.

Grapevine-Colleyville ISD serves most of the city, and Keller ISD serves the northwest. For restoration, that detail only matters in that families at several addresses may share one dispatch line.
