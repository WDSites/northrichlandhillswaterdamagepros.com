Creek water and drain backups come first in Haltom City

Big Fossil Creek drains 74.7 square miles and flows southeast through Richland Hills and Haltom City before reaching the West Fork Trinity River. On September 7, 1962, it produced the largest flood in Richland Hills since at least 1900, according to the U.S. Geological Survey. That history is why Haltom City homeowners should ask about drainage before they assume a wet floor is only a plumbing problem.

When storm runoff or creek water enters a house, the water is usually contaminated. Our crews follow the contaminated-water process described on the flood damage restoration page, and they start with safety, extraction and sanitizing before any drying begins.

What to do when water is spreading in Haltom City

If a drain is backing up or water is entering from outside, treat it as an emergency. Do not wade through it, and call the utility or 911 for any gas or electrical risk. Read emergency water removal if a pipe burst.

Stop the source

Close the main water valve if a pipe is the source. If water is coming in from outside, do not try to stop it by hand.

Power and safety

Keep out of flooded rooms, and cut power at the panel only if you can reach it standing on dry ground.

Protect what you can

Move documents, electronics and photos to higher shelves and pull small rugs out of the water.

Then call the line

Tell the dispatcher whether the water came from a pipe, a drain or outside, since each path changes the plan.

What the city says about its water, sewers and drainage

Since the late 1980s, Haltom City has purchased all of its drinking water from the City of Fort Worth. The system has 12 million gallons of storage and an average daily demand of 8 million gallons. The sewer division cleans more than half a million feet of sanitary sewer main every year.

The stormwater division clears open waterways, flood control ponds, bridges and culverts and cleans storm sewer pipes and inlets. Public Works takes calls at 817-834-9036. If a street drain or culvert is clogged outside your house, report it to the city, and call us for water that has already entered.

A city surrounded by neighbors

Haltom City is surrounded almost entirely by Fort Worth, North Richland Hills, Watauga and Richland Hills, and it sits about six miles from downtown Fort Worth. It was incorporated on July 5, 1949, so many of its houses are older than neighboring suburbs. Birdville, the first county seat of Tarrant County, is a historic community in the area, and Birdville ISD serves most of the city.

Older housing stock means more chances for failing supply lines and aging drains, so a crew may recommend a plumbing inspection after a loss. If your water is from a slow leak, water damage cleanup and mold remediation cover the follow-up.
